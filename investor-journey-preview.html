About the asset

Keizersgracht 250 is a fully refurbished Grade A office asset on one of Amsterdam's most iconic canals. The property spans 4,820 m² across seven floors, currently let to seven professional-services tenants on staggered leases averaging 7.2 years.

A 2024 refurbishment delivered EPC A+ certification, new HVAC, and a tenant-exclusive roof terrace. The asset is offered as a stabilised core investment.

Frequently asked

What happens after I submit an NBO?

Your non-binding offer is reviewed by the seller's advisor. If shortlisted, you'll be invited to the binding phase with a formal data room and a target exclusivity window of 4–6 weeks.

Can I share the IM with my analyst or lender?

Yes — the NDA covers your internal team and advisors. Please keep distribution limited to named individuals at your firm and its mandated financiers.

How do I withdraw from the process?

Just let Mark know. Your access will be revoked and all confidential materials should be destroyed per the NDA.
